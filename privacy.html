Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us at TrueFinds

1. Information We Collect

TrueFinds is a personal Pinterest affiliate marketing automation tool. We collect and process the following types of information:

Account Information

  • Pinterest account credentials (encrypted and stored securely)
  • Profile information (username, email)
  • Authentication tokens for API access

Usage Data

  • Pinterest board and pin information (for organization purposes)
  • Performance metrics (impressions, clicks, conversions)
  • Posting schedules and automation settings
  • Affiliate link performance data

Technical Data

  • Device information and IP address
  • Browser type and operating system
  • Application usage patterns and errors

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • Service Delivery: To provide automated Pinterest pin creation and scheduling services
  • Performance Optimization: To track affiliate link performance and improve conversion rates
  • Account Management: To organize and manage your Pinterest boards and content
  • Analytics: To generate reports on engagement and earnings
  • Security: To protect your account and prevent unauthorized access
  • Service Improvement: To enhance features and user experience

3. Data Storage and Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information:

  • Encryption: All sensitive data is encrypted using AES-256 encryption
  • Secure Storage: Credentials are stored in encrypted databases
  • Access Control: Strict access controls and authentication requirements
  • Regular Audits: Periodic security audits and vulnerability assessments
  • API Security: Secure API communication with Pinterest and Amazon

4. Third-Party Integrations

TrueFinds integrates with the following third-party services:

Pinterest API

  • Used for pin creation, board management, and analytics
  • Data is processed according to Pinterest's Terms of Service
  • We access only your own pins and boards as authorized

Amazon Associates Program

  • Used for affiliate link generation and commission tracking
  • Product research and price monitoring
  • Commission and earnings data processing

AI Services (DeepSeek, GLM)

  • Content generation for pin titles and descriptions
  • Image generation for pin visuals
  • No personal data is shared with AI services beyond content requirements

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share data only in the following circumstances:

  • Service Providers: With trusted third parties who assist in operating our service
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law or to protect our rights
  • Business Transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets
  • With Your Consent: When you explicitly consent to sharing

6. Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Remember your preferences and settings
  • Analyze website traffic and user behavior
  • Improve application performance and features
  • Maintain session security

You can control cookie settings through your browser preferences.

7. Your Rights and Choices

You have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction: Update or correct inaccurate information
    • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal data
  • Export: Receive your data in a portable format
  • Opt-out: Disable data collection and tracking
  • Complaint: File a complaint with data protection authorities

To exercise these rights, please contact us at privacy@truefinds.com

8. Children's Privacy

TrueFinds is not intended for children under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

9. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place to protect your data in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

10. Policy Updates

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by:

  • Posting the new policy on our website
  • Sending an email notification (if you've provided consent)
  • Updating the "Last Updated" date below

Your continued use of TrueFinds after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact Information

If you have questions about this privacy policy or our data practices, please contact us:

  • Email: privacy@truefinds.com
  • Website: https://truefinds.com
